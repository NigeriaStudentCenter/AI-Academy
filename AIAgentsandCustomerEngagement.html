Chapter 01

The Customer Is Changing

Modern customers increasingly expect immediate answers, personalised experiences, multiple channels, simple transactions, proactive assistance and 24/7 availability.

Traditional model

Customer → Website → Contact Centre → Agent → Department → Manager

Every additional hand-off can create delay, repetition and friction.

Agentic model

Customer → AI Agent → Information + Systems + Actions → Resolution

When judgement, empathy or authority is required, the agent escalates to a human.

The objective is not necessarily to eliminate people. The objective is to eliminate unnecessary friction.
Chapter 02

What Exactly Is an AI Agent?

A basic AI system may generate an answer. An AI agent is designed to pursue a goal within defined boundaries.

Goal
Context
Reasoning
Tools
Action
Feedback

Imagine a customer saying, “My order hasn't arrived.” A simple chatbot might direct the customer elsewhere. An agent could identify the customer, locate the order, check delivery status, communicate the result, initiate an approved resolution and record the interaction.

The difference is agency. The system is not merely producing language. It is participating in a business process.

Chapter 06

AI Agents in Customer Service

The strongest early use cases are usually repetitive, rules-based and information-intensive.

Strong candidates

Order tracking, appointment information, account enquiries, product information, returns guidance, FAQs, troubleshooting and onboarding.

Human-led situations

Serious complaints, vulnerable customers, high-value disputes, unusual circumstances, ethical decisions and complex negotiations.

The goal

Let AI handle routine work so customer service professionals can concentrate on high-value human interactions.

Chapter 14

AI Agents & the Future of Jobs

A better question than “Will AI take jobs?” is “Which tasks will AI take, and which new tasks will emerge?”

Routine tasks may decline

Repetitive data entry, basic information retrieval, simple status enquiries and routine scheduling.

New responsibilities emerge

AI agent supervision, conversation design, agent performance analysis, workflow design and AI governance.

Job redesign

Chapter 15

Risks, Ethics & Governance

Accuracy

Agents must not confidently provide incorrect information.

Privacy

Customer information must be protected and appropriately governed.

Security

Connections to business systems must be carefully controlled.

Bias

AI-supported decisions should be monitored for unfair outcomes.

Transparency

Customers should understand when they are interacting with AI where appropriate.

Accountability

Organisational responsibility does not disappear when a task is delegated to AI.

Delegating a task to an AI agent does not delegate organisational responsibility.

Chapter 19

Practical AI Agent Prompts

Customer Service Agent

You are a customer service AI agent. Help customers resolve routine enquiries accurately and efficiently. Use only approved organisational information. If the information required is unavailable or the situation requires human judgement, explain this clearly and escalate the issue.

Sales Qualification Agent

Assess each incoming enquiry against the organisation's approved qualification criteria. Identify likely needs, summarise the opportunity and recommend whether the enquiry should be transferred to a human salesperson.

Customer Experience Analyst

Analyse the following customer interactions. Identify recurring complaints, unmet needs, points of friction and opportunities to improve the customer journey. Separate evidence from assumptions and prioritise findings by potential customer impact.

Agent Improvement

Review the previous AI-agent interaction. Identify where the agent performed well, where it failed to meet its objective and what changes to its instructions, knowledge or workflow could improve the next interaction.

Chapter 21 · Conclusion

The Organisation That Learns to Engage Intelligently

AI agents represent more than another technological development. They represent a change in how organisations can work.

The first era of digital transformation moved information online. The second connected people and systems. The next era is beginning to connect intelligence, action and relationships.

Customer engagement sits at the centre of this transformation. The organisations that succeed will not necessarily be those with the most AI. They will be those that understand where AI creates value, where humans create value, and how the two can work together.

The future is not simply about artificial intelligence. It is about intelligent engagement.

A final challenge

Take one customer interaction in your organisation and ask:

What is the customer actually trying to achieve?
Why is the current process difficult?
Which parts require human judgement?
Which parts could an AI agent safely handle?
What would an exceptional experience look like if humans and AI worked together?

That is where the journey toward agentic customer engagement begins.
