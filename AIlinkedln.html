Write a Standout LinkedIn Profile

Beginner~20 minChatGPT · Claude · Copilot · LinkedIn
What you'll be able to do: turn your CV into a LinkedIn profile that recruiters actually find and want to read — a magnetic headline, a warm "About", and the keywords that get you found.
The scenario: Sam's LinkedIn is just a list of job titles. Recruiters scroll past it, and he never shows up in their searches. A stronger headline, a human "About" section and the right keywords change who finds him — and what they think.
Use any free app: these prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot — pick whichever you like. Everything here works on the free version.

2A headline that gets clicks

Your headline is the first thing recruiters see. Make it specific and keyword-rich.

Write me 5 LinkedIn headline options for a [target role] targeting [industry]. Make them specific and keyword-rich - not generic "Aspiring" or "Hard-working". Under 220 characters each. My background: [paste 2-3 lines about you].
What a good result looks like: e.g. "Marketing graduate | Content & social media | Grew a student society's following 3x | Seeking junior marketing roles" - specific and searchable.
Refine it: reply — "Make option 2 punchier and add the top keyword recruiters search for this role."

3A warm, human "About" section

The About is your story. Make it real, not robotic.

Write a first-person LinkedIn "About" section (3 short paragraphs) based on my CV below. Show my value, a bit of personality, and what I'm looking for. No clichés or buzzwords. CV: [paste]
What a good result looks like: three short paragraphs that sound like a real person: what you do, a proof point, and what you're after.
Refine it: reply — "Make it warmer and add one specific achievement with a number."

4Get found in searches

Recruiters search by keyword. Add the ones that match your target roles.

From this job advert, list the keywords and skills I should add to my LinkedIn headline, About and Skills so recruiters find me. Job advert: [paste]
What a good result looks like: a clear list of terms to weave in truthfully - e.g. "content creation, Canva, social media analytics, copywriting".
Refine it: reply — "Which 5 of these matter most, and where exactly should each one go?"

Quick profile checklist

  • Clear photo and a background banner
  • Keyword-rich headline
  • A human "About" section
  • Experience with results, not just duties
  • Skills section filled with your target keywords
  • A tidy custom profile URL
Try this today (5 min): run the headline prompt and paste your favourite straight onto LinkedIn. You've just made your profile more searchable in two minutes.
Common mistake: stuffing your profile with buzzwords like "passionate, hard-working, results-driven". Recruiters ignore them - show specifics and results instead.
Privacy & ethics: keep personal contact details off the public profile; use LinkedIn's messaging. Only list true skills and real achievements.

Check your understanding

Q1. Why does your LinkedIn headline matter so much?

It's the first thing recruiters see and it affects whether you appear in their searches.

Q2. What should the "About" section do?

Tell your story with personality and proof - not repeat your job titles.

Q3. Why add keywords from job adverts?

Recruiters search by keyword; matching terms make you findable.
