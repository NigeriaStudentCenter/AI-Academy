Advanced Prompting & Iterative Refinement

Intermediate~25 minChatGPT · Claude · Copilot · Gemini
What you'll be able to do: go beyond one-shot prompts — use examples, step-by-step reasoning and refinement loops to turn a mediocre first draft into a polished, publication-ready output.
The scenario: The AI gave you a bland first draft of a client proposal, and you're tempted to rewrite it all by hand. Instead, a few advanced techniques get the AI to fix its own work in seconds.
Use any free app: these prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot — pick whichever you like. Everything here works on the free version.

1Three techniques that change everything

Show, don't just tell: give 1-2 examples of the style you want.
Ask for reasoning: add "think step by step" for complex tasks.
Refine in a loop: have the AI critique its own answer, then rewrite it.

2Example-driven (few-shot) prompt

Give the AI a model to copy so the output matches your voice.

Here are two examples of the writing style I want: [Example 1] [Example 2] Now write [what you need] in exactly that style, tone and length.
What good looks like: output that mirrors your samples' tone and structure, not a generic template.
Refine it: reply — "Match Example 2 more closely on sentence length and formality."

3Self-critique & rewrite

Let the AI grade and improve its own draft against your criteria.

Critique your last answer against these criteria: [clear, specific, persuasive, under 200 words]. List what's weak, then rewrite it to fix every point.
What good looks like: a short list of weaknesses followed by a noticeably tighter, stronger rewrite.
Refine it: reply — "Now cut it by 30% without losing the key argument."

4Step-by-step for complex tasks

For anything with logic or multiple parts, ask it to reason first.

Think step by step before answering. First outline your approach, then give the final answer. Task: [describe the complex task].
What good looks like: a brief plan, then a more accurate final answer because it reasoned first.
Refine it: reply — "Show me one alternative approach and which you'd recommend."

Check your understanding

Q1. What is "few-shot" prompting?

Giving the AI one or more examples of what you want so it matches them.

Q2. When should you ask the AI to "think step by step"?

For complex, multi-part or logical tasks — reasoning first improves accuracy.

Q3. What is the self-critique loop?

Asking the AI to grade its own answer against criteria, then rewrite it better.
