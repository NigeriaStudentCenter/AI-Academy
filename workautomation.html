Workflow Efficiency & Daily Operations

Intermediate~20 minCopilot · ChatGPT · Claude · Otter.ai
What you'll be able to do: use AI to clear the routine work that eats your day — email, meeting notes and reports — reclaiming hours every week.
The scenario: It's 4pm: your inbox is overflowing, you owe meeting minutes to three people, and the weekly report isn't started. Done by hand that's two hours; with AI it's twenty minutes.
Use any free app: these prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot — pick whichever you like. Everything here works on the free version.

1Where AI saves the most time

Email

Copilot in Outlook / Gemini in Gmail: summarise threads, draft replies.

Meetings

Copilot in Teams or Otter.ai: auto-notes and action items.

Reports

ChatGPT / Claude / Copilot: turn notes or data into a clean report.

2Email: summarise & reply

Paste a long thread and get the gist plus a draft.

Summarise this email thread in 3 bullet points, then draft a reply that [confirms the deadline / politely declines / asks for X]. Keep it professional and under 120 words. Thread: [paste]
What good looks like: a 3-bullet summary and a ready-to-send, on-tone reply.
Refine it: reply — "Make the reply warmer and add a clear next step with a date."

3Meetings: notes to actions

Turn messy notes into a summary and an action list.

Turn these meeting notes into: (1) a 4-line summary; (2) an action table with columns Owner, Action, Due date. Notes: [paste]
What good looks like: a crisp summary and a table where every action has an owner and a date.
Refine it: reply — "Flag any action with no owner and suggest who should take it."

4Reports: bullets to brief

Draft a structured report from rough notes or data.

Turn these bullet points into a structured weekly report with: Highlights, Risks, Next week's focus. Keep it under 250 words. Bullets: [paste]
What good looks like: a manager-ready report with clear headings instead of a wall of text.
Refine it: reply — "Add a one-line executive summary at the very top."

Check your understanding

Q1. Which tool gives automatic notes and actions inside a meeting?

Microsoft Copilot in Teams (or Otter.ai).

Q2. What two things should a good "notes to actions" output always include?

An owner and a due date for every action.

Q3. Why give the AI a word limit on a report?

To keep it concise and manager-ready instead of a long wall of text.
