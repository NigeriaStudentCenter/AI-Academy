Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Do I need access to Dynamics 365 Sales or Power BI already?

The course teaches practical, hands-on use of these tools. You'll get the most from it with access to Microsoft 365 Copilot and Dynamics 365 Sales through your organisation or a trial, but no prior CRM experience is required.

What does "ethical outreach" mean in this course?

Outreach built on a clearly defined ICP and genuine value proposition, respecting consent and compliance — not mass-blasting contacts or deceptive tactics dressed up as personalisation.

What is the capstone project?

You ship a lead magnet and capture flow, set up auto-nurture with Power Automate, manage the resulting opportunities in Dynamics 365 Sales, and build an executive dashboard covering pipeline, conversion and revenue.

Is this course for marketing, sales, or both?

Both, deliberately. The course follows one commercial lifecycle from first outreach to closed revenue, so marketing and sales content sit side by side rather than in separate tracks.

How long does this course take?

Four modules covering 19 lessons and labs, roughly 7 hours 11 minutes of content in total. Most learners work through one module per sitting.
