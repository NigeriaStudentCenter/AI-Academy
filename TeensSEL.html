Social-Emotional Learning — the skills school leaves out

A teenager will spend years being taught maths, science and grammar, and almost no time being taught how to understand their own feelings, read other people, work in a team, handle pressure, or make a hard call well. Those skills decide how the rest of it goes. This course teaches them directly.

CASEL framework7 modules+ stress & conflict resolution

It follows the CASEL framework (Collaborative for Academic, Social, and Emotional Learning) — the standard used by schools and researchers worldwide — plus two extras that matter enormously for young people: managing stress, and resolving conflict. Where the AI course trains how a learner thinks, this trains how they are — with themselves and with everyone else.
