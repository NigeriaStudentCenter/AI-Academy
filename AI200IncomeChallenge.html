Frequently asked questions

Do I need to already know how to code or use AI tools?

No. Every day's playbook assumes you're starting fresh with that day's tools — it walks you through exactly what to use and how.

Do I have to keep up every single day?

No. Days stay unlocked as they're released, so you can move at your own pace and revisit any day whenever you're ready for it.

What if I want to stop?

It's a monthly subscription with no lock-in — cancel anytime from your account and you won't be billed again.

Is this the same as The Hustle's "200 Ways" list?

No. The category themes are inspired by The Hustle's well-known research into AI money-making ideas, but every day's idea, playbook and execution steps in this programme are written fresh — this is a guided, daily system, not a copy of their list.

Which idea should I expect to actually make money?

That depends on you, your time and your market — this programme is built to help you test many ideas quickly and double down on the one that clicks, not to guarantee any single outcome.
