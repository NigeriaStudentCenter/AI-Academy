Strategic AI Tool Selection & Core Prompt Structure

Beginner~25 minClaude · Copilot · ChatGPT · Gemini
What you'll be able to do: choose the right AI tool for a task and structure a prompt with the R-C-C-O framework, so you turn a 15-page document into a one-page executive brief in minutes — not hours.
The scenario: Your team needs a 15-page remote-work policy turned into a one-page summary of compliance changes for the executives — and the meeting is in 20 minutes. Pick the wrong tool or a vague prompt and you'll waste the whole 20 minutes on a generic, useless summary.

2The R-C-C-O framework

Great prompts have four parts. Use this every time and your results jump in quality.

Role — who the AI should act as.
Context — the background and the goal.
Constraints — rules: length, sources, what to avoid.
Output format — exactly how you want it laid out.

3The executive-brief prompt

Attach or paste your document, then use this R-C-C-O prompt.

Role: Act as a Senior Operations Analyst. Context: I am attaching a 15-page internal remote-work policy. The executive team needs to understand what changed this year versus last year. Constraints: Use ONLY the provided text. Do not invent details. Keep it under 300 words. Flag any compliance risks or hard deadlines. Output format: An Executive Brief with (1) Key Policy Changes (3-4 bullets); (2) a table of Compliance Risks & Deadlines (Item, Risk Level, Deadline); (3) Recommended Next Steps (2 bullets). Document: [paste text or attach file]
What good looks like: a tight brief: "Core hours shifted 10-4 to 9-3; stipend up $500 to $750; security training now semi-annual," a risk table with dates, and two clear next steps.
Refine it: too dense? reply — "Condense each Key Change to one sentence, bold the key metrics, and add one line at the top naming the single biggest change."

Check your understanding

Q1. What do the four letters R-C-C-O stand for?

Role, Context, Constraints, Output format.

Q2. Which tool suits a long document already in Word/OneDrive?

Microsoft Copilot (or Claude for careful long-context reading).

Q3. Why include "use only the provided text" as a constraint?

To stop the AI inventing details (hallucinating) and keep it faithful to the document.
