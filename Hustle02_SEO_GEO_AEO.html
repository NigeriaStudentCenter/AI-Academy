1

What Is The Hustle? SEO, GEO & AEO Explained

The opportunity, in plain language
Explanation

People are increasingly asking AI systems questions like "Who are the best accountants for small businesses in London?" — and receiving an AI-generated answer, not just a list of links. SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) helps pages appear in search engines. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) focuses on content that directly answers real questions. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is the emerging term for improving how a business is represented in generative AI systems.

The terminology is still evolving. The business problem is simple: how can we help a business become easier for people — and increasingly AI systems — to discover, understand and trust? Your business is an AI-assisted search visibility service, not "I will write blogs using AI."

Avoid AI Slop — Quality Over Quantity

The most important lesson in this course
Explanation

AI makes it extremely easy to produce content — that doesn't mean businesses need a thousand generic articles. The objective is not MORE AI CONTENT. It's MORE USEFUL INFORMATION: relevant, accurate, original, evidenced, backed by real expertise and human review. Think AI-assisted quality, not AI-generated quantity.

AI draft → human review → fact check → publish

Test AI Discovery

Observed results, not permanent rankings
Explanation

Record whether the company appears, how it's described, what sources are referenced, and what competitors show up. Never treat one AI response as a definitive ranking — AI responses change, and different users can get different results.

Growth, Recurring Revenue & The Numbers

Revenue is not profit
Explanation

Growth path: One audit → One client → Monthly content → Monthly monitoring → Multiple clients → Specialist niche → Productised service → Small agency. Specialising (e.g. "AI Search Visibility for UK Recruitment Companies") makes your offer easier to explain and market. Illustration: 5 clients × £750/month = £3,750/month, or 10 × £1,000 = £10,000/month — these are illustrations, not guarantees. Revenue − Costs = Profit: software, AI subscriptions, freelancers, website costs, taxes all come out first.

Don't Do This

Avoid these entirely
  • Promise you can guarantee a particular ranking or position inside ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI system
  • Publish fabricated information
  • Create fake reviews or invented expertise
  • Invent statistics
  • Copy competitors' content
  • Publish hundreds of meaningless AI-generated articles
  • Manipulate information deceptively
  • Claim an AI system uses a particular ranking factor without reliable evidence
  • Tell a client that one test proves permanent visibility

The most valuable skill isn't generating content — it's understanding information, evidence, customers and business objectives.
