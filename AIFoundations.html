Seven short, focused lessons — each one building directly on the last.

What AI Actually Is

A plain-language grounding in what AI, machine learning and generative AI are — and how they differ from the science-fiction version. You'll leave with an accurate mental model, not a hyped-up one.

By the end of this course

You'll be ready for everything that follows.

AI Foundations is deliberately short and practical. It exists to get you to a genuinely useful, confident starting point — fast.

  • Explain, in plain language, what AI can and cannot reliably do.
  • Write a clear, well-structured prompt that gets a useful answer first time.
  • Recognise the signs of an AI hallucination and know how to check it.
  • Apply a simple responsible-AI checklist before sharing AI-assisted work.
  • Follow safe data-privacy habits when using any AI tool.
  • Move into Course 2, Essential AI Tools, with real confidence.

Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any technical background?

No. AI Foundations assumes no prior experience with AI, coding or technical tools. Every concept is explained in plain language first.

How long does this course take?

Most learners complete the seven lessons and the practical task in a few focused hours, spread across as many sessions as you like.

Is there an assessment?

Yes — a short practical task at the end, marked to confirm you can apply what you've learned before moving on to Course 2.

What do I take after this course?

AI Foundations leads directly into Course 2 of the Academy, Essential AI Tools, where you put these foundations into practice across ten leading AI platforms.

Is this course included in the BSOE Certificate?

Yes. Completing AI Foundations counts toward the full BSOE AI Academy Certificate of Achievement, awarded on completion of the full pathway.
