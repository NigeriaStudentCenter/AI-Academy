Part 1

Your learner journey

Ten simple steps from signing in to celebrating a win.

  1. Sign inUse your academy account and keep your password private.
  2. Start HereRead the welcome, academy rules and safety information.
  3. Choose a coursePick a course that matches your level or goal.
  4. LearnRead, watch, listen and practise.
  5. Do the activityComplete a quiz, task, project or reflection.
  6. SubmitSend your answers or upload evidence.
  7. Wait for the resultA quiz may be marked quickly. Practical work may need staff approval.
  8. Check progressLook at your points, level, badge and next step.
  9. Join the communityAsk useful questions, share wins and help safely.
  10. Keep goingTry the next activity and build a record of real progress.
A good learner does not rush.
Take time to understand the task, check AI answers and save evidence of what you did.

Read, ask, help and celebrate safely.

Motivation

A challenge or encouraging message.

Question

A clear request for help.

Announcement

Official academy information.

Evidence of a real achievement.

How to make a good post

  1. Write a short, clear title.
  2. Explain what happened or what help you need.
  3. Choose the correct category.
  4. Check spelling and facts.
  5. Do not share passwords, addresses, telephone numbers, bank details or private medical information.
  6. Be kind. Do not copy, insult or embarrass another person.
  7. Select Save and wait for the post to appear.
Points are not given for noise.
Many short posts, repeated messages or copied answers should not earn points. Helpful, safe and approved contributions may earn points.

Part 6

Submitting quizzes and evidence

Show what you learned so your work can be checked.

For a quiz

  1. Open the correct quiz from the course page.
  2. Check your name and academy account.
  3. Answer every question you can.
  4. Select Submit only when you are ready.
  5. Read the result. If you did not pass, review the lesson before trying again.

For practical work

  1. Read the task and success rules.
  2. Create the work yourself, using approved tools.
  3. Save evidence such as a file, screenshot, link, short video or reflection.
  4. Open the Submit Evidence form from the course page.
  5. Choose the correct course and activity.
  6. Upload or link the evidence and explain what you did.
  7. Select Submit and wait for staff feedback.
One activity, one real submission.
Do not submit the same activity many times to collect extra points. The academy should check for duplicates.

Good evidence answers three questions

1
What did you do?

Describe the task and the tool you used.

2
What changed or improved?

Show a result, time saved, problem solved or skill improved.

3
What did you learn?

Explain one thing you now understand or can do.

Part 7

How points work

Points show approved learning, participation and results.

CourseLessons, quizzes, practical work and course completion.
CommunityUseful questions, answers and resources.
EventsAttend and reflect.
WinsReal results and projects.
Current position
The Leaderboard can show real points now, but trusted staff normally enter or change the points after checking your work. A quiz score does not automatically reach the Leaderboard unless the academy has built and switched on the correct automation.
Future automatic system
When the planned system is active, a quiz can send your score to SharePoint. A flow can check the activity, stop duplicates, calculate points and update your total. Practical work and community contributions still need approval.

Your four point pots

  • Course Points — lessons, quizzes, practical work and course completion.
  • Community Points — approved useful questions, answers and resources.
  • Event Points — attendance and approved event reflections.
  • Win Points — quick wins, projects and capstones.
Total Points = Course Points + Community Points + Event Points + Win Points

Awarded points = round((your quiz score ÷ maximum quiz score) × points available)

How the Top 5 should work

  • Learners are sorted by approved Total Points from highest to lowest.
  • Only real, checked work should be counted.
  • Copied work, spam, duplicate submissions and unsafe behaviour should not earn points.
  • A tie rule should be published. A good rule is higher Course Points first, then the earlier date the score was reached. Shared rank is also fair.
  • The academy may use monthly or term leaderboards so new learners also have a chance.
  • You can ask for a score review if you believe there is a mistake.
How to win the right way.
Learn well, complete practical work, save evidence, help responsibly, attend events and keep going. Quality matters more than quantity.

How course pages link to points

The Activity ID joins the right task to the right scoring rule.

1. Course card
2. Lesson page
3. Activity
4. Submit
5. Check
6. Completion
7. Points
8. Progress

What is happening behind the page

  • A course card opens the correct SharePoint course page.
  • The course page contains the lesson and buttons for the correct quiz or evidence form.
  • Each activity has a unique Activity ID.
  • The Activity ID tells the system which course, points and pass rule belong to the task.
  • One approved Activity Completion record is saved for your submission.
  • Your approved points are then added to the Leaderboard.
  • The progress page can show your level, badge and next course.
Why links must be correct.
Always open the activity from the course page. A random or old form may not contain the correct Activity ID and may not connect to your progress.

Part 15

What can be automatic and what staff check

A safe system uses both computers and people.

Can be automatic

  • Multiple-choice quiz score
  • Duplicate check
  • Add approved points
  • Recalculate Total Points
  • Update level and rank

Needs a person to check

  • Practical work and evidence
  • Written reflection
  • Helpful community answer
  • Capstone or graduation
  • Mistakes and score appeals

When automation is switched on

  1. You submit a quiz or activity form.
  2. The system reads your learner account and Activity ID.
  3. It checks whether the same completion already earned points.
  4. For a quiz, it calculates the score and pass rule.
  5. For practical work, it sends the evidence to a tutor or moderator.
  6. After approval, it updates Course, Community, Event or Win Points.
  7. It recalculates Total Points and may update your level, badge and rank.
Do not assume automation is active.
Until the academy confirms that the flows are live and tested, expect staff to enter or approve points manually. A Microsoft Forms result by itself may not update the Leaderboard.

Part 16

Missing points and getting help

Use this checklist before reporting a problem.

  1. Refresh the pageThe list may need a moment to update.
  2. Check the activityMake sure you used the correct course link and Activity ID.
  3. Check submissionConfirm the form said your response was submitted.
  4. Check the pass ruleA failed quiz may award 0 until you pass.
  5. Wait for approvalPractical work, posts, events and projects may need a person to check them.
  6. Check for duplicatesA second copy of the same task may not earn more points.
  7. Save evidenceKeep a screenshot, file, link or submission date.
  8. Ask for supportUse Get Support and explain the activity, date and expected points.

A good support message

Subject: Missing points for AI Presentation Task
Course: AI Foundations
Activity: AF-03 Practical Task
Date submitted: 26 July 2026
Expected points: 15
What happened: My form was submitted, but the points are not showing.
Evidence: I have attached the confirmation screenshot.

Be patient and honest.
Do not change scores yourself, create extra records or ask for points you did not earn. A staff member should review the evidence fairly.
