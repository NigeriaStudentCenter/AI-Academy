Tailor Your CV to Beat the ATS

Beginner~25 minChatGPT · Copilot · Claude · Gemini
What you'll be able to do: upload your CV and a job advert into an AI tool, compare them against the job and person specification, get an honest upgrade, and format your CV to pass the ATS — on your own, in under 15 minutes.
An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software most employers use to scan and rank CVs against the advert before a human sees them.
The scenario: Ama applied to 30 jobs and heard nothing. Her CV is strong but generic, so each employer's ATS scores it low against the advert's exact wording and filters her out before a person ever reads it. The fix isn't a better CV — it's a CV matched to each advert, fast.

2Compare your CV to the job & person spec

Once both files are attached, paste this prompt. It produces an honest gap analysis.

Act as a recruiter and CV expert. I have uploaded my CV and the job description / person specification. Compare them and produce a table with these columns: Requirement | Essential or Desirable | Do I meet it? (Yes / Partly / No) | Evidence in my CV | What to add or reword. Be honest and specific. At the end, give me a match score out of 10 and the 3 biggest gaps to fix first.

3Get AI to upgrade your CV

Now turn the gaps into a stronger CV — honestly, using only your real experience.

Based on that gap analysis, rewrite my CV's profile summary and work-experience bullet points so they better match this role. Rules: - Use ONLY my real experience. Do not invent anything. - Weave in the advert's key terms where they are genuinely true for me. - Start each bullet with a strong action verb and add a result or number where I have one. Output as plain text I can paste into Word.

4Beat the ATS

Ask the AI to surface the exact keywords the ATS scans for, then format the CV so the ATS can read it.

From this advert, list the exact keywords and phrases an ATS will scan for that are missing or weak in my CV. For each, tell me where I can add it truthfully. Then give me an ATS-friendly formatting checklist for my CV.

ATS-friendly formatting checklist (apply these to your CV file):

  • Single-column layout — avoid tables, text boxes and multiple columns (many ATS can't read them).
  • Standard headings: Work Experience, Education, Skills.
  • Standard fonts (Arial/Calibri); no logos, icons or photos.
  • Put your contact details in the body, not in the header or footer.
  • Match the exact job title where it's genuinely true.
  • Include the advert's key phrases in your own true wording.
  • Spell out acronyms once, e.g. Search Engine Optimisation (SEO).
  • Save in the format the advert asks for (usually .docx or PDF).
Try this today (5 min): take one real advert, upload it with your CV, and run Step 2. Read the match score and the 3 biggest gaps. You've just seen exactly why you were or weren't getting callbacks.
Common mistake: letting AI add skills you don't have because they're in the advert. Delete anything untrue — it gets exposed at interview.
Privacy & ethics: don't paste your home address, phone or ID numbers into a public AI tool — your experience is enough. AI tailors how you present real experience; it never invents it.

Check your understanding

Q1. What does an ATS actually do?

It scans and ranks CVs by how well they match the advert, before a human sees them.

Q2. Why upload the job spec as well as your CV?

So the AI can compare the two and show exactly which requirements you meet, partly meet, or miss.

Q3. True or false: it's fine to add advert keywords even if they're not true for you.

False — only include skills and keywords that are genuinely true.
