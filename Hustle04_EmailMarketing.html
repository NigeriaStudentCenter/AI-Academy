1

What The Business Idea Means

Not another email-writing tool
Explanation

Build an AI-powered email marketing platform that helps businesses create, personalise, send, test and optimise email campaigns. The key opportunity is not simply to create another email-writing tool. The platform should help marketers answer:

  • Who should receive this email?
  • What should we say to them?
  • When should we send it?
  • Which subject line is most likely to get attention?
  • Which version performs better?
  • Which customers are becoming inactive?
  • Who is most likely to buy?
  • What should we send next?

The AI becomes the decision and optimisation layer around the email marketing process.

2

The Problem You Are Solving

Lots of data, one message for everyone
Explanation

Many businesses collect large amounts of customer information but still send broadly similar emails to everyone. This can lead to:

  • Low open rates
  • Low click-through rates
  • Poor conversion
  • Unsubscribes
  • Repetitive campaigns
  • Too much manual campaign preparation
  • Poor customer segmentation
  • Missed sales opportunities
  • Difficulty understanding campaign data

Your platform can help turn customer and campaign data into more targeted and personalised communication.

AI Campaign Generator & Personalisation

From brief to personalised message
Explanation

Campaign generator. The marketer enters: Product: AI training course · Audience: University students · Objective: Increase registrations. The system generates email subject lines, preview text, email copy, a call-to-action, and different versions for different audiences.

Personalisation. Instead of “Hello everyone,” generate messaging from the customer information available — e.g. “Hello Sarah, we noticed you've previously attended one of our digital skills courses...” It could consider: name, previous purchases, previous engagement, customer segment, location, interests, behaviour and stage in the customer journey.

The platform turns the prompt below into a structured workflow, so marketers don't have to write prompts manually.

Subject-Line Optimisation & Automated A/B Testing

Let the results choose
Explanation

Subject lines. The system generates several and helps marketers test them. For example: A — “Learn AI Skills in 30 Days” · B — “Your AI Skills Could Be Your Next Career Advantage” · C — “Ready to Become AI-Ready?” Then it tracks performance and identifies which approaches generate stronger engagement.

A/B testing. Instead of manually creating and comparing campaigns, AI could help test: subject lines, email length, headlines, images, calls-to-action, offers, send times, personalisation and content structure — and progressively learn from campaign results.

AI Campaign Analytics

Explain the numbers in plain English
Explanation

This is where the platform becomes much more valuable than a simple AI copywriting tool. The dashboard explains campaign results in plain English. For example:

  • “Your campaign generated a higher click-through rate among customers who had previously purchased within the last 90 days.”
  • “Engagement has declined among customers who have received more than five promotional emails this month.”

The AI should not simply display numbers. It should help marketers understand what the numbers mean and what they could investigate next.

Worked Examples: E-commerce & Training Provider

Same platform, different segments
Explanation

E-commerce. An online clothing business with 20,000 customers has purchase history and email engagement data. The platform identifies: Segment 1 — recent purchasers · Segment 2 — customers who haven't purchased for six months · Segment 3 — customers interested in a particular product category · Segment 4 — highly engaged customers. It creates different campaigns for each, so the business isn't sending the same message to all 20,000 people.

Training provider. The AI could identify students interested in AI, previous learners, inactive learners, people who started but didn't complete registration, and people who downloaded information but didn't enrol. Each group receives a different campaign.

Don't Do This

Avoid these
  • Build “just another AI email writer” — build the decision and optimisation layer around the campaign
  • Send the same message to everyone on the list
  • Ignore consent, customer preferences or unsubscribe requirements
  • Publish AI-generated content without human review — and make it clear when AI-generated content is being used
  • Make unsupported claims in campaign messaging
  • Process customer information without access controls, secure integrations and a data-retention approach
  • Show marketers raw numbers without explaining what they mean and what to investigate next
  • Try to build a huge platform first — start with one clearly defined problem
