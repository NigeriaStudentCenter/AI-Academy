Before you enrol

Frequently asked questions

Do I need coding experience to start?

The programme is an applied beginner-to-advanced pathway — Phase 1 starts with foundational AI concepts, and Python/application-building begins in Phase 2. Some comfort with technical concepts helps, but you don't need professional coding experience on day one.

Why doesn't Phase 4 have a stated phase gate?

Unlike the other phases, the source curriculum for Phase 4 (Agentic AI Systems) moves straight from Unit 9's portfolio projects into Phase 5 with no separate gate assessment listed — its four portfolio projects (Customer Support Agent, Sales Agent, AI Consulting Firm Simulator, Universal Enterprise Agent) serve as that phase's evidence instead.

What happens if I score below 20 on a capstone?

You revise and resubmit. The grade boundaries are designed around demonstrating a genuinely working, evidenced solution — not a single pass/fail attempt.

What is the "named human approval" requirement?

Any solution you build that is capable of acting on real organisational systems — sending something, changing a record, triggering a process — must have a named person sign off on it before completion. This is a core governance discipline of the programme, not a formality.

How is this different from the shorter role-based courses in the Academy?

Those courses build focused, tool-specific skill in a few hours to a few weeks. This is the Academy's flagship, 24-week applied pathway — culminating in a defended, enterprise-grade portfolio and a genuine progression from AI Practitioner to AI Innovation Strategist.
