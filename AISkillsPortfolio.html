Before you start

Frequently asked questions

What is a "Prove-It" task?

A specific, real deliverable at the end of each lesson — a brief, a refinement, an automation — that turns what you learned into evidence you can actually show someone, not just a completion checkmark.

What is the R-C-C-O framework?

The prompt-structuring framework taught in Lesson 1. You'll learn exactly what it stands for and how to apply it as part of the lesson itself, using it immediately to turn a long document into a one-page brief.

Do I need to already know AI tools?

No. Lesson 1 starts with the basics of choosing the right tool for a task before moving into prompt structure.

What do I walk away with?

A complete, employer-facing one-page AI portfolio, plus four other Prove-It artefacts along the way — a brief, a refinement, an automation, and a caught error with its safety rule.

How is this different from AI Foundations?

AI Foundations builds broad AI literacy across seven lessons. This is a compact, five-lesson sprint focused entirely on producing tangible proof of your skills — a strong complement to Foundations, or a fast option if you already have basic AI experience.
