Why this matters

A credential the job market recognises — not just BSOE.

AI skills are everywhere on job listings. A Microsoft certification is proof an employer can verify independently, on top of everything you build in the Academy.

Enterprise scale

Microsoft Azure is one of the world's leading enterprise cloud platforms, and Azure OpenAI is how a huge number of organisations deploy generative AI safely and at scale.

Beyond the chatbot

As companies move from experimenting with AI to shipping real products, they need engineers who can build secure, retrieval-grounded, multi-agent systems — not just write a good prompt.

Third-party validation

The Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate credential is independently issued by Microsoft — evidence you can put on your CV and LinkedIn that speaks for itself.

Built to transfer

Every section here is hands-on and capped at two hours, built around real labs in real Azure services — so what you learn transfers directly into a working environment.

Before you enrol

Frequently asked questions

Do I need coding experience to start?

No. The path starts with AI-901 fundamentals, which assume no prior Azure or coding experience. Later stages introduce SDKs and code samples in JavaScript, Python and .NET, so basic familiarity with any programming language helps as you progress — but it isn't required to begin.

What is the AI-103 exam?

AI-103, "Developing AI Apps and Agents on Azure," is Microsoft's official certification exam for this pathway. Passing it earns the Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate credential.

Does BSOE cover the cost of the exam?

This pathway prepares you fully for the exam through hands-on labs and an official exam-prep session, but you sit Microsoft's AI-103 exam independently once you feel ready.

How is this different from AI Foundations or the AI Toolbox course?

AI Foundations and the AI Toolbox build broad, practical AI literacy across many tools. The Azure AI Engineer pathway is a specialised, certification-focused track that builds on that foundation toward one specific, independently-verified Microsoft credential.

How long will the full pathway take?

Every section is capped at two hours by design. Pace yourself against the learning goal you set in the orientation step — most learners move through one stage at a time rather than trying to complete all four at once.
