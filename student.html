Questions

Is this cheating?

No. It's a study aid and tutor — it helps you understand, plan, research and improve your own work. It does not write assignments for you to submit.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. Scholarships, jobs, events and accommodation all search worldwide — just tell it your country, region and town.

Is the information current?

Yes. Each tool searches the live web in real time, so results are up to date. Always confirm details on the official link.

Which levels does the study help cover?

From GCSE and A-Level/BTEC through to Undergraduate, Masters and PhD — the depth adjusts to your level.
