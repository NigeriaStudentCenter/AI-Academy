Before you start

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the Legal AI Accelerator course?

The Legal AI Accelerator is the broader, 4-phase foundational program covering security, governance, prompting and supervised sign-off. This Fast-Track is a narrower, execution-focused cohort specifically on contract cycle-time reduction, going deeper on secure Azure OpenAI retrieval over your own legal repositories. If you're new to AI-assisted legal work, the Accelerator is the better starting point.

What is the interactive safety module?

Hands-on practice mitigating real risks in AI-assisted legal workflows, built around Microsoft's responsible conversational AI guidelines — not passive reading, but practising the mitigation techniques directly.

Does this replace legal training or supervision?

No. This is a skills-building program, not a substitute for formal legal training or your firm's own approval and oversight processes. Every AI-assisted output should be reviewed before it's relied upon.

What is a "playbook" in this context?

Your firm's own negotiation and redlining standards. Module 1 trains Copilot-assisted drafting and redlining against that playbook specifically, rather than generic, unguided edits.

How long does this program take?

Eight weeks across four modules, covering 15 lessons and labs and roughly 8 hours 55 minutes of core content — with the remaining time spent applying it to real contract work.
