Governance notes

Built to sit inside your compliance boundaries.

This isn't a bolt-on Week 4 topic — every governance requirement below is a defined output of the program.

Apply Microsoft Purview sensitivity labels and DLP across AI-assisted outputs.
Enforce least-privilege permissions and tenant filtering for every Copilot deployment.
Use only approved data sources; disable external connectors where required.
Document prompt templates and change controls in a PMO AI Playbook.
Establish usage telemetry to monitor how AI is actually being used.
Define a RACI for exceptions — who approves a deviation from the standard, and how.
Why this matters

Shadow AI use is the real PMO risk, not slow adoption.

Without a standard, every project manager prompts Copilot differently, with no shared library and no visibility for governance. This program exists to close that gap deliberately.

One shared prompting standard

A common prompt library and meeting-summary template mean consistent quality across the PMO — not one PM's lucky phrasing versus another's guesswork.

An auditable, documented rollout

A PMO AI Playbook with prompt templates and change controls means anyone can see exactly what's approved, and why, at any point.

Automation with proof of value

Meeting-to-action automation paired with a BI view of time saved turns "we use Copilot now" into a number leadership can actually see.

Governance built in from day one

Purview, DLP and least-privilege permissions are a defined Week 4 deliverable — so the rollout finishes secure, not fast-then-fixed-later.

Before you start

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the other role-based courses?

Most Academy courses are self-paced modules you work through in any order. This is an explicit 4-week program with a weekly deliverable, designed to be run as a standardised PMO rollout rather than browsed casually.

What is the PMO AI Playbook?

The documented output of this program: your prompt templates, change controls and governance checklist, assembled week by week so your PMO has a single reference for approved AI use.

Do I need Azure DevOps and Power Platform access to take this course?

Ideally yes, since Weeks 2 and 3 are hands-on with Planner, Azure DevOps and Power Platform. Without that access you can still complete the Copilot-focused exercises, but the automation and reporting deliverables need those tools.

What is the RACI for exceptions?

A defined responsibility model — who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed — for approving any deviation from the PMO's standard AI usage rules. You establish this in Week 4.

How long does this program take?

Four weeks by design, covering 11 lessons and labs and roughly 2 hours 39 minutes of core content — the rest of each week is applying it to produce that week's deliverable.
