Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Do I need technical or IT experience?

No. This course is built for administrative professionals using the Microsoft 365 apps they already work in every day. No coding or IT background is required.

Will this replace my judgement with automation?

No — the course is built around human approval at every stage. Copilot drafts, Power Automate reminds and routes, but a person always reviews and approves before anything is final.

Why does a course about admin work include data protection?

Because administrative professionals routinely handle confidential documents, communications and records. Sensitivity labels and DLP basics protect that information as more of it passes through AI tools.

What is the capstone project?

A designed department workflow: a Copilot meeting summary becomes validated minutes, which feed an action tracker, route through a Power Automate approval, and get filed in SharePoint with retention applied — ending in a simple dashboard of actions and time saved.

How long does this course take?

Five modules covering 23 lessons and labs. Most learners complete one module per sitting rather than the full course in one go.
