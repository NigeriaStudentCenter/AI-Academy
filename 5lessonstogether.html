Why this track matters

The workplace is filling up with people who can chat to AI. Far rarer — and far more valuable — are the people who can wire AI into real systems and keep it safe. That's what you'll learn here.

You build, not just use

Connect data, call APIs, package Skills, design agents — real systems, not just prompts.

Safety is the edge

Guardrails, human-in-the-loop and data safety in every lesson — exactly what employers trust.

No-code and code

Every lesson has a hands-on "try it" for everyone, plus a "go deeper" for the technical.

The next decade won't reward people who ask AI questions. It'll reward the ones who can design the systems, connect the data, and set the guardrails — and prove it. That can be you.
