1

What Is The Hustle?

An AI document-processing and data-entry automation service
Explanation

Many businesses still have people reading documents, copying information and typing it into another system. That creates repetitive work — and it can create delays, inconsistent data entry and avoidable errors. Your business: help organisations automate processes where people currently receive a document, open it, read it, find specific information, copy it, enter it into another system, rename or store the document, notify somebody, and repeat.

Your AI-assisted system can potentially perform much of that workflow automatically. For example, an invoice pipeline:

Invoice received
AI reads invoice
Extract supplier, invoice no., date
Extract amount & VAT
Check required fields
Store information
Update spreadsheet / accounting system
Notify finance team

That is the business.

2

The Problem You Are Solving

Put a number on the paperwork
Explanation

Imagine a small business receives 200 invoices every month, and each takes about five minutes to process. That's 200 × 5 = 1,000 minutes — roughly 16.7 hours every month. If part of that process can be automated, the business may be able to redirect staff time toward more valuable work.

The opportunity is therefore not "I can use AI." It is: "I can identify repetitive document work and build an automation that reduces manual processing." That is a business proposition.

4

Find The Repetitive Task

One question, then ten follow-ups
Explanation

When talking to a business, ask: "What documents do your staff repeatedly receive, read and manually enter into another system?" Then dig into how many arrive each week, who processes them, how long each takes, what's extracted, where it's entered, what happens when information is missing or wrong, where the originals are stored, who checks it, and what happens after processing. You're looking for a process.

AI Tools
ChatGPT / Claude for role-playing the discovery callNotes app / Word
Practical Exercise

Map The Automation Opportunity

Worked example: recruitment CVs
Explanation

A recruiter manually reads a CV for the candidate's name, email, phone number, location, qualifications, experience and skills — then copies it into an applicant tracking system or spreadsheet. As a workflow:

CV received
Document stored
AI extracts relevant information
Information structured into fields
Validation
Database / CRM updated
Recruiter notified
CV stored against candidate

You've just turned a manual administrative task into an automated workflow.

Handle Documents Responsibly

Avoid these
  • Promise 100% accuracy — extraction can be wrong, so a human must handle exceptions and missing or incorrect data
  • Process CVs, identity documents, claims or health information without the client's authority and a clear data-protection basis (for UK/EU clients, that means UK GDPR)
  • Send a client's real documents to an AI service without checking where the data goes and how it is stored
  • Remove the validation step to make the demo look faster
  • Test on live client documents before you've tested on samples or dummy data
  • Sell "AI" — sell the reduced manual processing and the reliability of the whole workflow
