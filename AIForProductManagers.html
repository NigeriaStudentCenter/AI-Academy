Why this matters

Product decisions move at the speed of evidence.

Copilot and Power Platform don't replace product judgement — they remove the manual grind that keeps that judgement from being evidence-based.

Synthesis at speed

Copilot turns hours of interview notes and meeting transcripts into synthesised themes and actions in minutes, so prioritisation happens on evidence, not memory.

Prioritisation becomes defensible

Power Platform standardises feedback into consistent inputs and simple priority scores — a rankable backlog, not the loudest voice in the room.

Requirements stay linked to evidence

PRDs and user stories trace back to the research that justified them, so an executive reviewing the roadmap can see the "why," not just the "what."

Customer trust protected by design

Sensitivity labels, DLP and human-in-the-loop review are core modules here, not optional extras bolted on afterward.

Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a product management background?

This course assumes you're already working in or moving into a product-facing role and understand basic concepts like a backlog, roadmap and PRD. The Copilot and Power Platform tooling itself is taught from Beginner level up.

Does the AI decide what to prioritise?

No. Power Platform standardises inputs and computes simple priority scores to make trade-offs visible — the ranking decision and final call still belong to you.

What is the capstone project?

A finance policy change affecting product behaviour: you plan discovery, synthesise evidence, write a PRD and user stories, prioritise MVP scope, build an outcome-based roadmap, define KPIs and risks, and present an executive recommendation — all with a maintained validation trail.

How does this protect customer data?

Module 4 is dedicated to sensitivity labels, DLP, Power Platform environment strategy, human-in-the-loop review and documenting AI usage — treated as core skills throughout, not just one module you skim.

How long does this course take?

Five modules covering 24 lessons and labs, roughly 11 hours 14 minutes of content in total. Most learners work through one module per sitting.
