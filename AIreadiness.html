Frequently asked questions

Do I need technical or coding experience?

No. The assessment and Academy are designed to support beginners as well as people who already use AI. Technical ideas are introduced in practical, understandable stages.

How much does the Academy cost?

Membership is £6.50 per month. Learning is online, and you can start anytime from anywhere.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Learners who meet the completion requirements receive a certificate awarded by the British School of Outdoor Education.

Does the assessment guarantee a job or business income?

No. The assessment is an educational self-evaluation. It identifies development priorities but does not guarantee employment, promotion, income or business results.

Are my answers stored?

Your assessment answers are saved only in your current browser so you do not lose progress. They are not sent to BSOE by this webpage. Contact details are sent only if you choose to use the separate information form.
