The 90-Day AI Readiness Playbook

Your 30-60-90 plan to go from AI curious to AI capable — for a learner or a small business.

Roadmap30 · 60 · 90 daysNo vendor required

Becoming "AI-ready" isn't a single leap — it's 90 days of small, deliberate steps.

This playbook lays out a simple three-month path. Everything here can be done with the free tools and skills taught in the Academy — no vendor, no big budget. Follow it for yourself, or adapt it for a business you're helping.

Days 1–30 · Build the foundations

Weeks 1–2 · Learn & audit
  • Learn the basics: prompting, tool selection, data safety (Academy Lessons 1–4).
  • Audit where AI could help you most — list the repetitive tasks that eat your time.
  • Pick your everyday tools (a chat AI, a canvas) and get comfortable.
Outcome: a clear picture of where AI will save you the most time.
Weeks 3–4 · First real wins
  • Automate 2–3 routine tasks (email, notes, reports) with prompts you save.
  • Start a simple "prompt library" of what works.
  • Practise fact-checking and keeping private data out of public AI.
Outcome: real time saved, and safe habits from day one.

Days 31–60 · Build capability

Weeks 5–6 · Connect & structure
  • Connect AI to your own data safely (Advanced Lesson 1 · Connectors).
  • Build a reusable Skill / Custom GPT that follows your rules (Advanced Lesson 3).
  • Learn how APIs work so you can go from using AI to building with it (Lesson 2).
Outcome: AI that works with your real information, your way.
Weeks 7–8 · Cowork & refine
  • Co-create documents and tools in a canvas (Advanced Lesson 4).
  • Turn your best workflows into repeatable templates.
  • Write up your wins as short case studies (proof for a CV or a client).
Outcome: repeatable AI workflows and evidence you can show.

Days 61–90 · Scale safely

Weeks 9–10 · Agents & guardrails
  • Design a simple agent for a multi-step task (Advanced Lesson 5).
  • Add guardrails: spend caps, step limits, human approval for risky actions.
  • Test it on real work and watch it carefully.
Outcome: a safe, useful agent you understand and control.
Weeks 11–12 · Consolidate & share
  • Combine everything into one governed workflow (the capstone).
  • Document it and share it with your team or community.
  • Plan the next 90 days — what to deepen, what to build next.
Outcome: you're genuinely AI-ready — and can help others get there too.

Build your own playbook — with AI

This is a template, not a rulebook. Use AI to tailor a 90-day plan to your exact goal. Try:

Act as an AI adoption coach. Build me a practical 30-60-90 day plan to become AI-ready for [my goal, e.g. "a small marketing business"]. Give weekly steps and a clear outcome for each phase, using free tools. Keep it realistic and beginner-friendly.

Understand the shape of a good roadmap, then let AI adapt it to anyone's situation.
