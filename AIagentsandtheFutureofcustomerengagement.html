The opportunity

The next advantage isn't knowing AI exists. It's knowing what to do with it.

AI is changing how organisations attract customers, answer questions, sell, support users and deliver services. That creates an opportunity for people who can connect AI capability to real customer and business problems.

For entrepreneurs, that can mean smarter services, new propositions and more efficient customer engagement. For students and graduates, it means developing practical evidence that can be taken into interviews, applications, placements and entrepreneurial projects.
