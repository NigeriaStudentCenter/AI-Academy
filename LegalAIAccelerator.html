Why this matters

Legal work can't tolerate a confident wrong answer.

Speed is worthless in legal practice if it comes at the cost of accuracy, confidentiality or professional responsibility. This program is built around that constraint, not in spite of it.

Zero-hallucination checks are the skill

Precision prompting and documented human-in-the-loop verification are core, graded practice here — not a footnote after the AI draft looks convincing.

Client confidentiality comes first

Purview sensitivity labels, DLP and eDiscovery readiness are covered in Weeks 1–2 — before a single prompt touches real client data.

A supervisor stays in every loop

First-pass drafting, contract redlining and clause extraction always end in supervisor review and sign-off — never an unsupervised final output.

Built around real legal workflows

Four-element prompting in Word and Outlook, contract review, and Copilot Studio agents over internal knowledge reflect how legal teams actually work.

Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Does this course replace legal training or bar admission requirements?

No. This is a skills-building curriculum in AI-assisted legal work — it does not substitute for formal legal education, qualification, or your jurisdiction's admission and professional conduct requirements.

Does the AI draft final legal documents?

No. AI produces first-pass drafts, research summaries and clause extraction — every output is reviewed and must be approved by a supervising qualified lawyer before it is relied upon or sent to a client.

What is HITL, and why is it central to this course?

HITL means human-in-the-loop: a person verifies AI output before it's trusted. Phase 3 is built entirely around precision prompting and documented HITL verification, because legal work cannot tolerate a confident but wrong answer.

Why do supervisors lead Phase 2?

Purview sensitivity labels, DLP configuration and eDiscovery readiness carry real confidentiality and compliance consequences, so this phase is explicitly supervisor-led rather than self-paced.

How long does this program take?

Eight weeks across four phases, covering 12 lessons and labs and roughly 5 hours 19 minutes of core content — with the remaining time spent applying it under supervision.
