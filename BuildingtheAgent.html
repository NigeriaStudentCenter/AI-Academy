1 · Short Teaching Video / HeyGen Script

How to Build an AI Agent

Recommended length: 8–10 minutes.

Welcome to Module 5 of the BSOE AI Academy.

You have discovered the customer opportunity and designed the experience. Now we move into the build stage.

Think of an AI agent as several connected layers. First is its purpose. Then its instructions: the rules that tell it how to behave. Then its knowledge: the approved information it can use. Then its context: what it needs to understand the current customer situation.

Next come tools. Tools allow an agent to retrieve information or perform actions. But tools create responsibility. If an agent can change an order, issue a refund or update a record, that capability must be deliberately controlled.

Workflow turns a vague instruction into a repeatable process: identify intent, gather context, retrieve information, reason within permitted rules, take an authorised action and confirm the result.

Finally, build the boundaries. What happens when information is missing? What happens when the agent is uncertain? What happens when the customer asks for something outside its role? What happens when a human must take over?

A good prototype is not judged by how impressive it sounds. It is judged by whether it completes the intended customer task reliably and safely.

Build narrow. Test deliberately. Expand only when evidence supports expansion.

3 · Interactive Example

Build a Delivery Support Agent

Weak build

“You are a helpful customer-service assistant. Answer all delivery questions.”

No defined knowledge boundary, tool rules, authentication requirement or escalation logic.

Controlled build

“You are a delivery-status support agent. Help authenticated customers understand current order status using approved information and the order-status tool. Do not invent status, promise dates not present in the source, or change orders. Escalate disputes and unresolved exceptions.”

Customer saysBuild behaviour
Where is order 4821?Authenticate → validate reference → use status tool → explain.
Can you change the address?Check authorised capability; otherwise explain limitation and escalate.
It says delivered but I do not have it.Follow missing-delivery process; do not invent a resolution; escalate where required.
4 · Case Study

University Student Support Agent

A university wants to reduce repetitive enquiries about timetables, assessment submission rules, student-service processes and appointment booking.

Phase 1

Answer approved questions about assessment submission procedures and direct students to authoritative guidance.

Phase 2

Connect current faculty guidance, regulations and approved student-service information.

Phase 3

Add appointment booking only after permissions, confirmation and escalation have been tested.

CapabilityBuild decisionReason
Answer approved procedural questionsBuildHigh-volume and bounded.
Predict whether a student will passDo not buildRequires judgement and may be high impact.
Retrieve appointment availabilityPotentialUseful controlled tool.
Cancel appointmentLaterConsequential action needs stronger controls.
Exceptional support concernEscalateHuman support required.
Case lesson: Add capability according to customer value, risk and evidence — not merely because the technology makes it possible.
