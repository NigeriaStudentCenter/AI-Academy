Build Your Employer-Facing AI Portfolio

Capstone~30 minChatGPT · Claude · Copilot · Canva
What you'll be able to do: combine everything into a one-page AI portfolio and a CV "AI Skills" section that proves — with real case studies and results — that you can use AI to solve business problems. This is the artifact that earns your certificate.
The scenario: You're interviewing for a role that lists "AI efficiency" as a preferred skill. "Proficient in ChatGPT" on a CV means nothing to a hiring manager. A one-page portfolio showing real AI workflows, with verified results, makes you unforgettable.
Use any free app: these prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot — pick whichever you like. Everything here works on the free version.

1Gather your four wins

You already built the raw material in Lessons 1–4: an executive brief, a refined draft, an automated workflow, and a caught error. Those are your four case studies. Have them ready.

2Build the one-page portfolio

Turn your four wins into a professional, shareable page.

Turn these four AI projects into a professional one-page portfolio: [Project 1] [Project 2] [Project 3] [Project 4] Use: a headline about my AI capability; four case studies each as Problem / Tool / What I did / Result; and a short "AI Skills" summary. Keep it concise and employer-facing.
What good looks like: a clean, scannable page: a strong headline, four tight case studies with results, and a skills summary.
Refine it: reply — "Make the results more quantified and the headline more confident."

3Write the CV "AI Skills" section

A punchy, honest block for the top of your CV.

From those four projects, write an "AI Skills" section for my CV: 4 bullet points, employer-facing, each with a tool and a result. Confident but honest.
What good looks like: four strong bullets naming real tools and outcomes — not vague "familiar with AI" lines.
Refine it: reply — "Tighten each bullet to one line and lead with the result."

4Prepare your 60-second pitch

Be ready to present your portfolio out loud.

Using my portfolio, write a confident 60-second spoken pitch answering "How do you use AI in your work?" — natural, not scripted, ending on a result.
What good looks like: a smooth, confident answer that names real examples and lands on an outcome.
Refine it: reply — "Make it sound more like how I'd naturally speak and cut it to 45 seconds."
Make it shareable: paste the portfolio into a free Canva one-pager or a simple document, export as PDF, and add the link to your CV and LinkedIn. A clickable portfolio beats a claim every time.

Put this on your CV & say it in interview

CV bullet
"Built and presented a portfolio of AI-driven business solutions — from executive-brief automation to verified, compliant reporting — demonstrating measurable efficiency gains of [X hours/week] and positioning AI capability as a core professional strength."
Interview answer (STAR)
"Rather than just listing 'AI skills', I built a one-page portfolio of four real projects with verified results and walked the interviewer through it. It turned an abstract claim into concrete proof — and it's the reason the conversation shifted from 'can you use AI?' to 'how soon can you start?'."
Swap the [bracketed] figures for your own real results — bold is great, but every number must be true for you.

Check your understanding

Q1. Why does "Proficient in ChatGPT" carry little weight with employers?

It's an unverified claim — there's no evidence behind it. A portfolio provides proof.

Q2. What structure makes a strong case study?

Problem, Tool, What I did, Result (with a real number where possible).

Q3. What should you do with your finished portfolio?

Export it as a PDF/link and add it to your CV and LinkedIn.
