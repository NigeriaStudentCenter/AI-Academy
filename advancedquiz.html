First — the words you'll need, in plain English

Agent
An AI that doesn't just answer once — it's given a goal and takes several steps on its own to reach it. Like sending a helper to "organise my party": they make a list, book things, and check it's done.
Workflow
The set steps a task follows, in order. Like a recipe: do this, then this, then that.
Tool call
When the AI uses something outside itself to get a step done — search the web, look in a database, send an email. Like a chef reaching for a specific kitchen gadget.
Connector
A safe link that lets the AI see your real data (files, email, a database) instead of you pasting it. Like giving a trusted helper a key to one cupboard — not the whole house.
Human-in-the-loop (HITL)
A checkpoint where a person must approve before the AI does something risky or costly. Like a "are you sure?" that a manager has to sign off.
Guardrails
The safety limits you set so the agent can't go too far — spend caps, step limits, "ask a human first" rules.
