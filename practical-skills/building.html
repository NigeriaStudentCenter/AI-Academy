Common questions

Know what you are joining.

Can this course be completed online?

The knowledge lessons, technical diagrams, simulated practice and workbook activities are designed for online learning. Some physical skills require separate qualified supervision.

Does the course include a practical assessment?

Online exercises and project evidence support formative assessment. Actual workplace competence must be established separately where required.

Is it an official Pearson qualification?

No. Pearson vocational subject areas are used for curriculum reference; training and proposed completion certification are independent BSOE offerings.

When can I pay and access the full course?

Checkout is intentionally disabled until verified course prices, payment integration and secure learner access have been configured. The free preview is available now.

Will an AI tutor answer questions?

The existing lesson preview has guided assistance. A live Azure AI tutor is planned but is not yet integrated into the published service.
