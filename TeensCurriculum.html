Applied AI, taught as an honest study co-pilot — not a shortcut.

A four-track programme that teaches teenagers the prompt frameworks, working routines and checking habits that turn a chatbot into a real learning partner — plus the judgement to use it without cheating themselves. Every learner starts with a mandatory safety module before touching a single tool.

The Academy Code

Four rules, graded on every submission — the whole point is honesty, not shortcuts.

  1. Add your value. Change, cut or build on at least 40% of anything an AI gives you. If a learner can't explain a sentence in their work, it doesn't go in.
  2. Check before you trust. No fact from an AI enters the work without a second source the learner found themselves — a textbook, a named website, a teacher.
  3. Guard what's private. Never paste full essays, other people's personal details, a home address, or photos of real people into a public AI tool.
  4. Say when you used AI. Tell the teacher how AI helped — "to plan", "to check my grammar", "to make flashcards". Honesty is the whole point.

Earning the certificate

Awarded for all four badges plus an accepted capstone.

AI READY

Track 1

Explains what a language model does and one weakness; knows and applies the Academy Code; Myth-Buster posted with two independent sources.

PROMPT BUILDER

Track 2

Can write a strong prompt from a blank page; has a working study-tutor prompt they can explain; shipped one custom assistant.

BUILDER

Track 3

Shipped a working no-code tool a real user used unaided; delivered spec, instructions, user guide and a live demo.

CREATOR

Track 4

Can build and refine a layered image prompt; produced the 3-piece set, each item labelled "Made with AI"; can explain likeness and consent.

Every learner gets a Microsoft 365 account on enrolment, including Microsoft Copilot — signed in with the Academy account, a learner's own work stays inside the school's tenant, the safe default for anything involving their own writing.
