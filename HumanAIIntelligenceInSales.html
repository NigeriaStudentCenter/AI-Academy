Before you start

Frequently asked questions

How is AI actually used in this course?

Differently from most Academy courses. Here, AI presents and delivers each session — the video and the guided workbook activity — rather than being a tool you're learning to operate. The skill you're building (reading people, persuasion, trust) is entirely human.

Do I need to already work in sales?

No. Session 1 starts from the fundamentals — why people buy at all — before building toward more advanced topics like psychology and body language.

What's the difference between the Seven Steps of Selling and the modern consultative approach?

Both are covered across the course — the traditional Seven Steps in the earlier sessions, and the modern, customer-focused consultative approach in Sessions 2 and 5 — so you understand both schools of thought rather than only one.

Is my workbook private?

Yes. Your answers save automatically to your own private workspace as you go — nobody else sees them unless you choose to share them.

Can I type or speak my answers?

Both are supported. Use whichever is faster for you in the moment.
